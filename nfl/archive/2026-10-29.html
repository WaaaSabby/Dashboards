Method

Matchup first: DvP HQ lenses and the exploit board, then the player's own projection and volume, then tonight's injury news. Overs only. Starters only, except the non-starter tickets. Backups don't inherit a DvP grade unless their snaps back it. Every ticket is one book. Since it's one game, slips are SGP estimates: legs multiplied at that book, then cut about 10% per added leg and 5% per same-team pair (TD tickets cut 5% per leg, sacks 3%).
